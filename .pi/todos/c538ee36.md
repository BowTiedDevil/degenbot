Remove `assert!(!self.running)` from `register_pool` and `register_path` in:
- `UniswapEngine` (`uniswap_engine.rs`)
- `V2BlockEngine` (`v2_block_engine.rs`)
- `V3BlockEngine` (`v3_block_engine.rs`)
- `V4BlockEngine` (`v4_block_engine.rs`)

Remove the `running` field and `start()`/`is_running()` methods from the sub-engines (V2BlockEngine, V3BlockEngine, V4BlockEngine). The top-level `UniswapEngine` may still track whether the pump is active for its own coordination, but registration must never be gated.

Remove `freeze()` from the PyO3 `UniswapArbEngine` wrapper entirely (no backward compat shim).

Remove `initial_solve()` — it's an artifact of the phased start, replaced by eager solving in `register_and_solve_path`.

**Decisions**: 
- Registration is always-on
- `freeze()` removed entirely
- `initial_solve()` removed — replaced by eager solving
- Sub-engines have no `running` concept
