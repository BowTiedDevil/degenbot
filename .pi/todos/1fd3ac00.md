## Problem
`rust/CONTEXT.md` documents `solve_dirty` as computing the incremental diff **and** sending a `ResultBatch` (see the "solve_dirty" and "Eager Processing" entries). The implementation no longer does this — solve and send were decoupled, with sends driven by a 50ms debounce timer. Pure doc drift, not a code bug.

## Evidence (verified during the Rust solver review)
- `solve_dirty` solves only: `rust/src/optimizers/uniswap_engine/event_routing.rs:106`. Explicit comment at `solver_dispatch.rs:90`: *"no compute_diff_and_send here — the pump controls when batches are dispatched"*.
- Separate `send_result_batch` (→ `compute_diff_and_send`, `result_channel.rs:76`): `event_routing.rs:134`.
- The pump drives a **50ms debounce** between solve and send (`DEBOUNCE_MS = 50`, `rust/src/optimizers/uniswap_engine_pump.rs`):
  - `solve_dirty` at pump `:507` (top-of-loop coalescing)
  - `send_result_batch` on debounce expiry at `:518`, on block-boundary flush at `:550`
  - block-boundary `finalize_if_dirty` calls both at `:698-700`
- CONTEXT.md's "Eager Processing" entry describes only the solve coalescing at the top of the pump loop and **does not mention the debounce timer / send decoupling at all**.

## Scope
- `rust/CONTEXT.md` — update the "solve_dirty", "Eager Processing", and "UniswapEnginePump" entries.
- Reflect that: solves are coalesced at the top of each pump iteration; sends are coalesced per log-burst via a 50ms debounce, and force-flushed on block boundaries and on 60s-timeout recovery.
- Ensure cross-references to `send_result_batch` / `compute_diff_and_send` are accurate.
