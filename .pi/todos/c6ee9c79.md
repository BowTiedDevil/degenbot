After fixing the V3-V3 encoder to use reverse-order direct custody, 89/99 V3-V3 paths
now simulate successfully. 13 remain failing:

- 6 empty reverts — likely auto-pay trying to transfer WETH the executor doesn't have
- 4 IIA (Insufficient Input Amount) — V3's balance-delta check still fails
- 3 TF (TransferFailed) — ERC20 transfer revert

These may be:
1. Paths where the V3 pool is owed a non-WETH token (auto-pay tries to transfer from empty balance)
2. Paths where zfo flag or swap amount is incorrect
3. Paths with uint128 overflow in amount encoding

Low priority — the core 2-hop V3-V3 encoding is working.
