## Resolution

All 2-hop V4-involving encoders had the same `V4_SETTLE` (0x55) vs `V4_SETTLE_ALL` (0x57) bug. `V4_SETTLE` is a raw `PM.settle()` that only credits sync+transfer — it does NOT auto-settle nonzero deltas. After V4_TAKE with explicit amounts, rounding residuals (1-2 wei) remain unsettled → `CurrencyNotSettled`.

### Fix
Added `V4_SETTLE_ALL` at the end of every V4_UNLOCK inner block across all 2-hop encoders:
- `V4-V3`: Both native-ETH and ERC-20 branches
- `V3-V4`: Both native-ETH and WETH branches  
- `V4-V2`: Both native-ETH and V2_SWAP_CALC branches
- `V2-V4`: Both native-ETH and ERC-20 branches
- `V4-V4`: Already fixed in TODO-9fe35071

### Results
- Before: ~4,700 CurrencyNotSettled across V4-V4 (4,304), V4-V2 (208), V2-V4 (959), V4-V3
- After: **0 CurrencyNotSettled** across all path types
- sim-ok: V4-V2 (75), V2-V4 (75), V4-V4 (38) — all failures are now token-level (OVERFLOW, IBIT max wallet) or phantom-profit (V4_TAKE_DELTA non-positive delta)
