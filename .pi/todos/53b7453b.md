## Background
The bot crashed during `build_paths` because the Rust engine's `verify_on_register` feature fires rapid `eth_call` RPCs during mass pool registration, overwhelming the local node and causing connection failures.

The previous "fix" swallowed `RuntimeError` when it contained RPC failure strings. This is wrong — real verification failures should crash loudly.

## What to fix
- Revert exception swallowing in `build_paths()`
- Address the root cause: too many concurrent/blocking RPC calls during verification

## Options to investigate
1. Rust-side: Add retry with backoff in verification RPC calls
2. Python-side: Throttle registrations with async sleeps to avoid RPC overload
3. Rust-side: Distinguish "RPC transport error" from "data mismatch" in errors

- `examples/eth_backrun_v2_v3_v4_rust.py`
- `rust/src/optimizers/uniswap_engine/py_binding.rs` (verification code)
