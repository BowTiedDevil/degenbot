## Problem
`rust/CONTEXT.md`'s `ResultBatch` entry documents profit-threshold filtering but only mentions the **lower** bound. The engine also filters an **upper** sanity bound (`max_profit`) to suppress likely-defect / scam-token profits, and this is undocumented.

## Evidence (verified)
- `set_profit_thresholds` const fn sets both `min_profit` and `max_profit` (`rust/src/optimizers/uniswap_engine/result_channel.rs`).
- `compute_diff_and_send` filters every category with `r.profit > self.min_profit && r.profit < self.max_profit` (`result_channel.rs:76` — fresh, updated, expired, retain, and delivered-add loops).
- Defaults: `min_profit = U256::ZERO`, `max_profit = U256::MAX` (`rust/src/optimizers/uniswap_engine/mod.rs`, `new()`).

## Scope
- `rust/CONTEXT.md` — extend the `ResultBatch` entry (and/or the `set_profit_thresholds` mention) to document:
  - `max_profit` is an upper defect/scam-token guard (paths with profit ≥ max_profit are excluded from `delivered` and batches).
  - The effective window is `[min_profit, max_profit)` — i.e. strict `>` / `<`.
  - Defaults: `min_profit = 0`, `max_profit = U256::MAX` (upper guard disabled by default).
