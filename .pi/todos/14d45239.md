## Q&A Complete

**Q**: What storage slots does `eth_simulateV1` need to pre-populate to replicate the effect of `cmd_executor.initialize()` for injected runtime bytecode?

**A**: Three slots across three contracts. `compute_simulation_warmup_slots()` in `cmd_stream.py` computes them for any executor address.

### What `initialize()` does (net effect)

```
1. msg.value=2 ETH         →  executor receives 2 wei
2. IWETH.deposit(value=1)  →  executor gets 1 wei WETH, pays 1 wei ETH  
3. Inside PM.unlock():
   V4_SYNC(WETH)                             — PM tracks WETH reserves
   ERC20_TRANSFER(WETH, PM, 1)               — PM gets 1 wei WETH
   V4_SETTLE                                  — +1 WETH delta recorded
   V4_MINT_COMPACT(WETH, self, 1)            — erc6909_balanceOf[executor][weth_id] = 1
```

### Storage Slot Formulas (VERIFIED ON MAINNET)

| Slot | Contract | Formula | Value | Purpose |
|------|----------|---------|-------|---------|
| `WETH.balanceOf[executor]` | WETH9 | `keccak256(executor . 3)` | 1 wei | Warms WETH ERC20 balance (slot 3 — WETH9 has name/symbol/decimals at slots 0-2) |
| `PM.erc6909_balanceOf[executor][weth_id]` | PoolManager | `keccak256(keccak256(executor . 4) . weth_id)` | 1 wei | Primary slot from V4_MINT_COMPACT |
| `PM.erc6909_balanceOf[executor][native_id]` | PoolManager | `keccak256(keccak256(executor . 4) . 0)` | 1 wei | Pre-warms native ETH ERC6909 (NOT in initialize()) |

Where:
- `weth_id = uint160(WETH_ADDR)` per CurrencyLibrary.toId()
- `native_id = uint160(address(0)) = 0`
- WETH9: `balanceOf` at mapping slot 3 (name=0, symbol=1, decimals=2)
- PoolManager: `balanceOf` at mapping slot 4 (owner=0, pendingOwner=1, isOperator=2, protocolFeesAccrued=3, balanceOf=4)

### PoolManager Storage Layout (C3 Linearization, Verified)

| Slot | Variable | Source |
|------|----------|--------|
| 0 | `owner` | `Owned` |
| 1 | `pendingOwner` | `Owned` |
| 2 | `isOperator` | `ERC6909` |
| 3 | `protocolFeesAccrued` | `ProtocolFees` |
| 4 | `balanceOf` | `ERC6909` |
| 5 | `allowance` | `ERC6909` |

Initial assumption was `balanceOf` at slot 1 (naive: first mapping in ERC6909). Verified wrong — actual slot is 4 due to `Owned` and `ProtocolFees` preceding `ERC6909.balanceOf` in the C3 linearization.

### Gas Impact

Per cold SSTORE (0→nonzero): 22,100 gas
Total savings: ~48,000–66,000 gas for V4-hybrid paths (2-3 slots)
